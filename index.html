What we predict

Real AI outputs from our models — across every dental radiology workflow.

Every image below is an actual Rayoscan model output. Each detection is explainable, visually grounded, and report-ready.

RayoNova · OPG
Full Arch OPG Analysis

Panoramic detection across all 32 tooth positions

  • FDI tooth numbering — every visible tooth labeled by position (Maxilla + Mandible)
  • Impacted tooth detection with angulation classification (mesioangular, vertical, horizontal)
  • Caries detection and surface localization from panoramic view
  • Nerve proximity and relation mapping for surgical planning (e.g. inferior alveolar nerve)
  • Color-coded overlays per finding type with confidence scoring
RayoApex · RVG · 95% confidence
Caries Detection & Tooth Segmentation

Severity-graded caries with full tooth and pulp mapping

  • Four-tier caries severity grading — Severe, Moderate, Mild, and Enamel-level caries
  • Multi-surface localization — Occlusal, Mesial, and Distal surfaces identified per tooth
  • Tooth segmentation boundary drawn per tooth unit
  • Pulp chamber detection for proximity-to-nerve assessment
  • Per-detection confidence score reported inline
RayoApex · RVG
Implant Detection

Implant localization with segmentation

  • Detects dental implants on periapical RVG images
  • Segmentation boundary drawn around each implant body
  • Handles multiple implants in the same frame
  • Works across varying radiographic densities and angulations
RayoApex · RVG · 96% confidence
RCT Tooth Detection

Root canal fill and restoration identification

  • Gutta percha fill material detected within canal space
  • Crown and restoration (coronal seal) identified separately
  • Full tooth segmentation boundary alongside RCT findings
  • Confidence scoring per anatomical structure identified
RayoSpectra · Bone Loss
Bone Loss Analysis

CEJ–ABL measurement and severity staging

  • Cemento-Enamel Junction (CEJ) and Alveolar Bone Level (ABL) landmark detection
  • Bone loss distance measured and displayed per tooth
  • Stage-based severity interpretation (mild / moderate / severe)
  • Visual measurement lines overlaid on the radiograph for clinical review
